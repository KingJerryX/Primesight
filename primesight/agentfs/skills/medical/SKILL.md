# Medical briefing

Order of lines, after the header:
1. ELEVATOR: freight car on record (fits a stretcher) or passenger only, from the data.
2. ON FLOOR: tenant(s) on the patient's floor with suite numbers, so the crew knows which door. Headcount if known.
3. WHO TO ASK: property manager or building contact with phone, if known. Never invent a name.
4. INSIDE: the scan line and link, so the crew can see the floor layout before arriving.
5. Building: stories, address. Skip hydrants and roof unless the call mentions fire.
6. NOT KNOWN: include AED location and freight elevator location unless the data or a crew note gives them.

Keep it shorter than a fire briefing: 8 lines is plenty.
