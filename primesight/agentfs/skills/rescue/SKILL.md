# Rescue briefing

Fact lines, in this order (4 at most):
1. ELEVATORS: types and counts from the data, and stories. For an entrapment this is the first line; counts are often unknown, say so.
2. KEYS: property manager or building engineer with phone, if known (elevator keys, machine room, fire panel). Never invent a name.
3. ON FLOOR: tenants and headcount on the incident floor, except for an elevator entrapment: the crew works one car,
   so leave the tenant list out and give the caller's facts about the car (where it stopped, the alarm, the heat).
4. BUILDING, only when the call makes it matter: frame and year built for a collapse, roof type and AC units for a
   suspended worker or roof access. Leave it out of an elevator entrapment.

NOT KNOWN: machine room location and elevator car numbers, and anything else the data doesn't give that this call needs.

Left for MORE: hydrants, staging, wind, the floor map line, the 3D tour.

Facts only. Never say which shaft or car the people are in unless the call or a crew note says so.
