# Security briefing

Order of lines, after the header:
1. PEOPLE: headcount on the incident floor and the floors next to it (NEARBY line), so command knows how many may shelter or flee.
2. ON FLOOR: tenants and suites on the floor. Flag vulnerable occupants (clinic, school, daycare) from tenant-risk.
   Never call a tenant a possible target because of who it is (religion, ethnicity, nationality, politics): crews act on
   where people are, not on guesses about motive.
3. EXITS: exit signs and pull stations seen in the scan, and walk distances from the scan start. Say these are from a 3D scan, not a plan.
4. STAGING: open lots from the aerial search, with distance and direction. Say "verify" (a lot may be in line of sight).
5. WHO HAS ACCESS: property manager with phone (badges, cameras, elevator recall). Security supervisor contacts are rarely in the data; say so if missing.
6. NOT KNOWN: camera locations, door locks, stair locations.

Never suggest tactics (entry points, routes to take). Facts only; command decides.
