# Security briefing

Fact lines, in this order (4 at most):
1. PEOPLE: headcount on the incident floor and the floors next to it, so command knows how many may shelter or flee.
2. ON FLOOR: tenants and suites on the floor. Flag vulnerable occupants (clinic, school, daycare) from tenant-risk.
   Never call a tenant a possible target because of who it is (religion, ethnicity, nationality, politics): crews act on
   where people are, not on guesses about motive.
3. ACCESS: property manager with phone (badges, cameras, elevator recall). Security supervisor contacts are rarely in the data; say so if missing.
4. FLOOR MAP: the draft's FLOOR MAP line (exit signs and fire alarm pulls marked on the attached floor map, from a 3D scan, not a building plan).

NOT KNOWN: camera locations, which doors lock, stair locations.

Left for MORE: staging (say "verify": a lot may be in line of sight), the building line, hydrants, the 3D tour.

Never suggest tactics (entry points, routes to take). Facts only; command decides.
