# Hazmat briefing

Order of lines, after the header:
1. TENANT RISK: tenants flagged for possible hazardous materials (labs, chemical, battery, medical gases), with floor. Say "(from name only)" when inferred.
2. TENANT INDUSTRIES across the building, from the data.
3. ON FLOOR and people on floors near the incident floor.
4. OPEN LOT for staging with distance and direction (aerial search, verify). Note that wind direction is not in our data: it goes on NOT KNOWN.
5. HYDRANTS: the two nearest.
6. INSIDE: the scan line and link.
7. NOT KNOWN: wind, what is actually stored, plus the usual unknowns.
