# Hazmat briefing

Fact lines, in this order (4 at most):
1. HAZARD: tenants flagged for possible hazardous materials (labs, chemical, battery, medical gases), with floor. Say
   "(from name only)" when inferred. If none is flagged, give TENANT INDUSTRIES from the draft instead.
2. PEOPLE: headcount on the incident floor and the floors next to it.
3. WIND from outside-scout: where it comes from, mph, and where gas or vapor drifts (live reading, give its time).
   If outside-scout says wind is unavailable, wind goes on NOT KNOWN.
4. STAGING with distance and direction (S on the approach map, aerial search, verify).

NOT KNOWN: what is actually stored, and shutoff locations (and wind, if unavailable).

Left for MORE: hydrants, the building line, the floor map line, the property manager, the 3D tour.
