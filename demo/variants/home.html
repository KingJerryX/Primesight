Our mission is to tell every crew what is inside the building before they reach the door.

Three views of one address

Known before the door

A crew usually learns a building on arrival: which entrance, which stairs, who is on the floor. Primesight reads the 3D scans, aerial photos and tenant records for that address and sends the answer as one text while the truck is still moving. Where the data runs out, it says so.

Imagery: Esri, Maxar. Map data: OpenStreetMap, OpenFreeMap. Aerial photos: Vexcel. 3D scans: Matterport. Building records: CoStar.
