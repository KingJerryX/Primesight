First responderPrimesightDrill

Do you know the way out?

Walk a real office floor against the clock. Then the lights go out and something goes wrong.

    1. You get seconds to look around this floor.
    2. Drag to look. W or ↑ walks forward, A D turn, click the floor to walk to a spot.
    3. Notice the exits, the exit signs, the pull stations, which way you're facing.
    4. When the clock runs out the screen goes black and you answer questions from memory.

    Click the plan to drop a pin.

    Answer from memory.
